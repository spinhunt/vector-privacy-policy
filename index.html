Privacy Policy for Vector

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

Vector ("Vector," "the Extension," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Vector handles information when you use the Vector browser extension.

1. Information We Collect

Vector is designed to operate within your web browser. We do not sell, rent, or otherwise monetize personal information.

Depending on how you use the Extension, Vector may access information displayed on the webpage where the Extension is being used. This information is accessed only to provide the Extension's functionality.

Vector does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to us or to third parties unless such functionality is specifically required for a feature described in the Extension.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed by Vector is used only to provide the Extension's intended functionality.

We do not use information accessed by Vector for purposes unrelated to the Extension's single purpose.

We do not use information accessed by Vector to:

  • Determine a person's creditworthiness;
  • Make lending decisions;
  • Sell personal information;
  • Provide targeted advertising;
  • Build advertising profiles; or
  • Track users across websites for advertising purposes.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or transfer user data to third parties for their own purposes.

Vector does not transfer user data to third parties except where such transfer is necessary to provide a feature of the Extension and is consistent with this Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage

Vector is designed to minimize the collection and storage of user information.

Information processed by the Extension is handled only as necessary to provide its functionality. We do not maintain a remote database of users' personal information.

If information is stored locally by the Extension, such storage is used only to support the Extension's functionality.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Vector. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Vector is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Vector, applicable requirements, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted at this location with a revised Effective Date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Vector's privacy practices, please contact:

Email: spencerhunt6@gmail.com

9. Summary

In short, Vector is designed to respect user privacy. We do not sell user data, we do not transfer user data to third parties for their own purposes, and we do not use user data for purposes unrelated to Vector's single purpose. We also do not use user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
